Reel Editor Toolkit

Set up slot reels, paylines and wins visually.

A Unity Editor tool for 2D slot games, coming to the Unity Asset Store. Have a question before then? Write to me.

    The demo’s starting screen. Nothing pays.

    A replay in your browser of the demo scene that comes with the package: the same art and the same eight planned results. In Unity you press Play and click Spin.

    How it works

    Everything is set up in the Unity Editor. Your own script decides the results, the balance and the game features. The toolkit scrolls the reels, lands them on the result, works out the wins and shows them.

    1. Lay out the reels

      Open the Reel Editor window and set the number of reels and rows, the symbol size and the gaps. A preview shows in the Scene view, and one click generates the reels and a Spin button.

    2. Name the symbols and set what they pay

      Give each symbol a name and a sprite. Payout rows say exactly, between, or more, or less. Use one shared payout list or a list per symbol.

    3. Draw the paylines

      Draw fixed paylines on a grid, or switch to Anywhere Pays. Wins count from the left, and optionally from the right as well. A Wild stands in for the other symbols. Scatter and Bonus symbols call your own functions when enough of them land.

    4. Press Spin

      The reels accelerate, scroll and land on the result, with an optional bounce. Winning symbols light up, a win readout appears, and the Console explains line by line why a line paid or did not.

    What is in the package

    • Reel Editor window with a live preview and one-click reel generation.
    • Symbol Library with flexible payout rows.
    • Smooth spin with landing and bounce, and Inspector settings for speed and timing.
    • Fixed paylines or Anywhere Pays, counted from the left and optionally from the right.
    • Wild, Scatter and Bonus: a Wild stands in for the others, and Scatter and Bonus call your own functions.
    • Win feedback: highlights, an on-screen readout and a line-by-line Console report.
    • Hooks for your game logic: results from your own server or math engine, starting symbols, reel sets, and events for spin start and reel stop.
    • Demo scene, documentation and source code, with its own namespace and assembly definitions.

    What stays yours

    Reel Editor Toolkit is the reel machinery of a slot game, not a complete game. It does not keep a balance, take bets, produce the math results or run bonus games. Those parts are yours, and the toolkit gives your script the events and the win list it needs.

    Requirements

    Unity
    2022.3 or newer.
    Unity UI package
    Draws the Spin button. New projects already include it (com.unity.ugui).
    Input System package
    Optional. Supported if your project uses it (com.unity.inputsystem).
    Third-party components
    None. The demo art was made for this package.
